## **Information pursuant to Art. 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**

Below you will find information on the processing of your personal data by Helmholtz Zentrum München – German Research Center for Environment and Health (GmbH) (hereinafter "Helmholtz Munich") in connection with the use of the Oligo Designer Toolsuite Cloud, and on your rights under data protection law.

## **1. Purpose of data processing**

Personal data is processed to provide, operate, and ensure the functionality and security of the Oligo Designer Toolsuite Cloud service, to generate user-requested computational results, to prevent misuse, and to improve the service.

## **2. Categories of data processing**

The following personal data is processed by for the above-mentioned purpose:

- Pseudonymous user identifier obtained via Helmholtz Authentication and Authorization Infrastructure (AAI)

- Uploaded genome data (processed temporarily)

- Processed research outputs (e.g. designed oligonucleotides)

- Voluntarily provided data (e.g. feedback or contact requests)

## **3. Legal basis for data processing**

Processing of authentication data and research outputs is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Uploaded genome data may be processed based on consent pursuant to Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR where applicable.

## **4. Recipients of the data and third country transfer (other EU countries)**

Within Helmholtz Munich, access to personal data is limited to staff members who require it to provide and maintain the service. Personal data is not disclosed to third parties and no transfer to countries outside the EU/EEA takes place.

## **5. Duration of processing, deletion of data**

Personal data is stored only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. In particular:

- User identifiers and research outputs are deleted upon user request, after 12 months of inactivity, or at the latest after five years.

- Uploaded genome data is deleted immediately after processing.

- Data from anonymous users is deleted after 30 days.

## **6. Automated decision-making/profiling**

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling in accordance with Article 22 GDPR.

## **7. Rights of data subjects under the GDPR**

You have the following rights in connection with the processing of your personal data:

- In accordance with Art. 7 GDPR, you have the right to **withdraw** your **consent** to data processing (if you have given it to us) at any time. The withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

- In accordance with Art. 15 GDPR, you have the right to request **information** about the personal data concerning you that is processed by Helmholtz Munich.

- In accordance with Art. 16 GDPR, you have the right to demand the immediate **correction** or **completion of** incorrect or incomplete data stored by us.

- In accordance with Art. 17 GDPR, you have the right to request the **erasure of** personal data concerning you that is stored by us, unless the processing is necessary for exercising the right of freedom of expression and information, for compliance with a legal obligation to which Helmholtz Munich is subject, for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims.

- In accordance with Art. 18 GDPR, you can request the **restriction of** the processing of your personal data if you dispute the accuracy of this data or if the processing of this data is unlawful.
